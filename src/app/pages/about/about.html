Our Story

CliqOrbit was founded with a single conviction: that most businesses were leaving enormous value on the table because their digital marketing was reactive, fragmented, and opaque. We set out to change that.

Starting with a small team of programmatic specialists and data scientists, we built our own AI-driven bidding infrastructure from the ground up — one that could make smarter decisions in milliseconds and explain every one of them.

Today, CliqOrbit operates across 15+ countries, has delivered over 10 billion ad impressions, and works with 500+ clients ranging from ambitious startups to global enterprises. The mission hasn't changed: make every marketing dollar work harder.
